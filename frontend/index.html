💡 Parent Support Guide

How to Support Arjun at Home

Personalised tips based on Arjun's ADHD profile — reviewed by BrightPath's special educators.

⚡ ADHD — Daily Routines

✅ Keep learning sessions under 20 minutes with a 5-min break
✅ Use a visual timer (egg timer) so Arjun can see time remaining
✅ Same time, same place every day — routine is everything for ADHD
✅ Remove distractions: phone away, TV off, quiet room
✅ After a lesson, ask "What was your favourite part?" not "What did you learn?"

🏆 Motivation & Rewards

✅ Celebrate every small win — finishing a lesson is an achievement!
✅ Let Arjun see his BrightPath stars growing — it motivates him
✅ Reward with activities he loves after 3 completed sessions
✅ Never compare with other children — his journey is unique
✅ Say "I'm proud of how hard you tried" — not just results

📖 Reading Support (Dyslexia Tips)

✅ Read books aloud together — 10 mins in the evening
✅ Use your finger to track words while reading
✅ Allow extra time — never rush through text
✅ Use the 🔊 Read Aloud button in BrightPath lessons
✅ Try audiobooks for story-based learning

🌈 Emotional Wellbeing

✅ Check Arjun's mood log in BrightPath regularly
✅ On anxious or sad days — start with a Brain Game, not a lesson
✅ Talk about school feelings without judgement
✅ Use the Calm Breathing activity before homework
✅ Contact Ms. Sharma if mood is consistently low for 3+ days

📞 When to Contact the Teacher

🚨 Immediately: If Arjun refuses to attend school, shows extreme distress, or mentions being bullied.
⚠️ Within a week: If progress drops below 40% or mood is consistently sad/anxious for 3+ days.
✅ Monthly: Request a progress call to review Arjun's BrightPath report with Ms. Sharma.
